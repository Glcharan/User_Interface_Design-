About

BANGALORE!! Get ready to witness 4 POP STARS in your city after 10 successful shows across INDIA! The band is back with a bang with their INDIA TOUR and is all set to take the country by storm with their musical hits like Kya Hua Tera Wada, Aur Iss Dil Mein, Gulabi Aankhen, Lag Jaa Gale, and many more.
The band SANAM consists of Sanam Puri (lead vocalist/composer), Samar Puri (lyricist/lead guitar/composer), Venky S or Venkat Subramaniyam (bass guitar) and Keshav Dhanraj. They are well known for their renditions of old Classic Indian Bollywood songs and original music.
The band was also among India`s Top 10 Independent YouTube Channels in 2016, won the Best Music Content Creator Award at Social Media Summit & Awards in 2017, and also declared an artist of the month on MTV Beats for their new release "Sanam Mennu".
